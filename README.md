**GAME RUN INSTRUCTIONS**

To run the game:
* Make sure you have the "music.mp3" file installed.
* Make sure the "music.mp3" file is located in the data folder.
* Once those two steps are complete all you need to do is run the code.


How to play the game
* Once the game is loaded your shown a screen with your character standing on the dock.
* To wind your rod press the left arrow.
* To cast your rod press the right arrow.
* Now wait for a fish to bite your hook. 
* Once you have a fish on the hook you're shown the fishing minigame screen.
* In order to complete the minigame you need to keep the yellow circle inside the green rectangle.
* You do this by tapping the up arrow to bounce your character (similar to flappy bird).
* While your circle is in the rectangle your score increases, starting at 40%.
* If your circle is outside the rectangle your score decreases.
* If your score reaches 100% you catch the fish!
* If your score reaches 0% you lose the fish.
* You can see the information about the fish on the top left under the last catch section.
* Just above that is your money and number of catches.

This concludes how to setup and play Fishing Simulator. Enjoy!
